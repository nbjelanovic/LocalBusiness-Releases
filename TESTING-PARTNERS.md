# Testing Partners

Local Business Platform is looking for small-business owners, managers, and
bookkeepers who can evaluate real daily workflows without exposing production
business data.

## Businesses that may be a useful fit

- Retail stores and online merchandise sellers
- Bakeries, pizza shops, bars, flower shops, and other food or specialty shops
- Contractors, repair services, and field-service businesses
- Local delivery and cross-country trucking operations
- Professional and appointment-based services
- Small wholesalers, inventory-based businesses, and mixed product/service
  operations

Specialized or regulated businesses may require capabilities and compliance
review beyond the general application. Testing interest does not mean the
application is approved for a regulated workflow.

## Useful feedback

- Whether setup chooses a sensible template and module set
- Whether common tasks are easy to find and complete
- Whether touch-screen and point-of-sale layouts work at the intended screen
  size
- Whether documents, dashboards, and populated reports are understandable
- Whether backup, restore, export, permissions, and offline behavior are clear
- Whether terminology and translations fit actual business use
- Which missing workflow causes the greatest practical interruption

## Testing boundaries

- Use fictional records or a separately copied and sanitized test dataset.
- Do not use live payment credentials or irreplaceable business records.
- Do not post customer information, employee records, credentials, private
  reports, database files, backups, diagnostics, or screenshots containing
  sensitive data in public GitHub issues.
- A test build is not a production approval, compliance certification, or
  promise of ongoing support.

Public download and tester-enrollment instructions will be added only after an
exact package is approved for distribution.
